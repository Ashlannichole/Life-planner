🌱 Sprout privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Sprout is a planner. It keeps what you put into it so it can plan your days, and it doesn't sell, share or advertise with any of it.

What Sprout keeps

  • Your plan: tasks, routines, events, meals, check-offs, your garden and your wins jar.
  • Your settings, and birthdays or anniversaries you add.
  • If you turn on calendar sync in the iPhone app: the title, date and times of events from the calendars you choose (from a week ago to three months ahead), so your plan can work around them. Sprout also adds its own events to a calendar called "Sprout" on your phone; it never changes or deletes anything in your other calendars.
  • If you connect Apple Health in the iPhone app: a daily summary of sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, steps and calories. Sprout only reads these, never writes to Apple Health, and uses them only to suggest lighter days and show your numbers.

Where it's stored

Everything is saved on your device. If you create an account, your plan (including those daily Apple Health summaries and any synced calendar events) is also saved to your account so it syncs between your devices. Accounts are run on Supabase, which stores the data for us and doesn't use it for anything else.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders and the home-screen widget are set up on your phone from your own plan. Nothing is sent anywhere to make them work.

Purchases

Sprout Plus is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; Sprout never sees your card. We use RevenueCat to check whether your account has Plus, so RevenueCat receives your Sprout account ID and your purchase history from Apple, but not your plan.

What Sprout doesn't do

  • No ads, and no ad or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing your data.
  • No reading your contacts, photos or location, and only the calendars you choose.

Deleting your data

In Sprout, go to Settings → Account & devices → Delete account. That deletes your account and everything saved to it. Data saved only on a device goes away when you delete the app.
